# Code of Conduct

We want ALFRED to be a respectful, inclusive and constructive project space.

## Expected Behavior

- being respectful and constructive
- welcoming different levels of experience
- giving feedback that helps improve the project
- respecting privacy and personal boundaries
- focusing on ideas rather than attacking people

## Unacceptable Behavior

- harassment
- insults or personal attacks
- discriminatory language
- intimidation
- publishing private information
- mocking disability, health constraints or personal situations
- aggressive or dismissive communication

ALFRED includes themes related to emotional support, accessibility and human-centered AI. This project does not aim to replace human relationships, medical care or professional support.
